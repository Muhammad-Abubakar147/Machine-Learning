EDA with bivariate and multivariate is analysis which is done on two more than two columns is known as bivariate and multivariate EDA .

If we do analysis on two columns than analysis called as bivariate EDA .
if we do analysis on more than two columns than analysis called as multivariate EDA .



=> Bivariate EDA :

=> Analyzes the relationship between two variables.
=> Helps identify correlation, patterns, and dependencies.
=> Common techniques: scatter plots, correlation, and box plots.

=> Multivariate EDA : 

=> Analyzes three or more variables simultaneously.
=> Helps discover complex relationships and interactions among variables.
=> Common techniques: heatmaps, pair plots, and multivariate visualizations
